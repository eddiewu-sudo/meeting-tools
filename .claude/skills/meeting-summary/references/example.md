## ENGLISH

### 1. Basic Information
- Date: TBD
- Attendees: Karen (industry advisor, ex-P72), CMoney overseas BD team
- Topic: Overseas go-to-market priorities, pricing, and tiered delivery
- Meeting type: External advisory discussion

### 2. Summary
The discussion focused on CMoney's overseas expansion priorities, pricing, and delivery strategy. Karen advised shifting from broad outreach to about ten anchor accounts with deep partnerships, and noted pricing appears too low — with room to raise it for Tier 1 quant funds. The group agreed to tier delivery packages by client, revise the data dictionary before distribution, and avoid giving strategy recipes to Tier 1 to protect alpha. A direction also emerged to position Taiwan data as a leading indicator for US markets and to grow accounts via a SimilarWeb-style land-and-expand model.

### 3. Q&A Recap
- **Is the go-to-market broad or prioritized?** Response: near-term priority is quant funds (fastest signal validation); other types run on a separate track.
- **How active are BNY and P72 in Asian markets?** Status: `[to confirm]`; such multi-managers usually have APAC PMs in Hong Kong or other Asian offices.
- **Why does the BNY contact see CMoney as "small"?** Status: Karen to follow up for specifics.
- **How do clients access trial data?** Response: currently via API.
- **Can Karen install the CMoney apps herself?** Status: team to send iOS download links.

### 4. Key Data & Metrics
- Market-data history: 10+ years; alternative data (social / app / consumer): 2–3 years.
- Reference point: in 2016, with only 3 years of credit-card history, many quant teams chose to wait.
- Suggested anchor-account count: ~10.
- SimilarWeb / P72 reference: one login grew to a 500K–700K contract over several years.
- Data-dictionary revision deadline: before 6/5.

### 5. Discussion Points
- **Go-to-market:** quant-first is reasonable; also build a narrative for fundamental investors.
- **Data history:** short history is a real barrier; highlight the 10+ year market data, be patient on newer alt data.
- **Anchor clients:** focus on ~10 deep partnerships for higher value and faster feedback.
- **Pricing:** the BNY contact called pricing too low — signal of value; raise for Tier 1.
- **Documentation:** tier packages; lead the data dictionary with unique strengths, add an overview page, use weekly samples.
- **EDA & recipes:** Tier 1 redoes analysis internally; wide recipe distribution erodes alpha — exclude from Tier 1.
- **Cross-selling:** introduce alternative data into existing BNY / P72 market-data relationships.
- **Taiwan → US narrative:** frame Taiwan semiconductor behavioral signals as a leading indicator for US names (Nvidia, Intel).
- **SimilarWeb model:** land with one team, embed in their systems, expand across teams until it's a must-have.
- **Brand credibility:** "CMoney" can read as spam to Western audiences; include 全曜財經 and registration details in outreach.

### 6. Decisions
- Quant funds are the near-term priority; other types on a separate track.
- Raise pricing, especially for Tier 1.
- Tier the delivery package by client.
- Revise the data dictionary before 6/5.
- Exclude strategy recipes from Tier 1 packages.
- Include 全曜財經 and registration details in all outreach.

## 繁體中文

### 一、基本資訊
- 會議日期：待補
- 與會人員：Karen（產業顧問，前 P72）、CMoney 海外 BD 團隊
- 會議主題：海外 go-to-market 優先順序、定價與分級交付
- 會議性質：外部顧問討論

### 二、會議摘要
本次討論聚焦 CMoney 海外拓展的優先順序、定價與交付策略。Karen 建議由廣撒轉為鎖定約 10 家錨點客戶深耕，並指出定價偏低、對 Tier 1 量化基金有調升空間。與會者同意依客戶分級調整交付包、發送前改版資料字典，並避免向 Tier 1 提供策略 recipe 以保護 alpha。會中亦形成將台灣資料定位為美股領先指標，並以 SimilarWeb land-and-expand 模式擴大客戶內部採用的方向。

### 三、問答彙整（Q&A Recap）
- **Go-to-market 是廣撒還是有優先順序？** 回覆：近期優先量化基金（最快驗證訊號價值），其他類型走另一條軌道。
- **BNY 與 P72 在亞洲市場的活躍程度？** 狀態：`[待確認]`；此類多重經理人通常於香港或其他亞洲據點設有 APAC 投資經理。
- **BNY 窗口為何認為 CMoney「小」？** 狀態：Karen 將追問具體原因。
- **客戶目前如何取得試用數據？** 回覆：目前透過 API。
- **Karen 是否可自行安裝 CMoney App？** 狀態：團隊將提供 iOS 下載連結。

### 四、關鍵數據與指標（Business Metrics）
- 市場數據歷史：10+ 年；替代數據（社群／App／消費）：2–3 年。
- 參考點：2016 年信用卡數據僅 3 年歷史時，多數量化團隊選擇觀望。
- 建議錨點客戶數：約 10 家。
- SimilarWeb／P72 參考：由單一 login 於數年內成長至 500K–700K 合約。
- 資料字典改版時程：6/5 前。

### 五、討論重點
- **Go-to-market：** 量化優先合理；同時建立面向基本面投資人的敘事。
- **數據歷史：** 短歷史是真實障礙；突顯 10+ 年市場數據，對較新替代數據保持耐心。
- **錨點客戶：** 鎖定約 10 家深耕，提升價值與回饋速度。
- **定價：** BNY 窗口直言偏低，反映價值；Tier 1 應調升。
- **文件交付：** 依分級調整；資料字典先呈現獨特優勢、加入概覽頁、用週頻樣本。
- **EDA 與 recipe：** Tier 1 會自行重做；recipe 散播過廣侵蝕 alpha — Tier 1 不予提供。
- **交叉銷售：** 於既有 BNY／P72 市場數據關係導入替代數據。
- **台灣 → 美股敘事：** 以台灣半導體行為訊號作為美股（Nvidia、Intel）領先指標。
- **SimilarWeb 模式：** 由單一團隊切入、嵌入其系統、擴展至多團隊直至成為必需品。
- **品牌可信度：** 「CMoney」對西方受眾易被誤認為垃圾信；對外溝通加入 全曜財經 與註冊資訊。

### 六、決議事項
- 近期以量化基金為首要客群，其餘走另一軌道。
- 調升定價，尤其 Tier 1。
- 依客戶分級調整交付包。
- 6/5 前完成資料字典改版。
- Tier 1 交付包不含策略 recipe。
- 對外溝通一律加入 全曜財經 與註冊資訊。
